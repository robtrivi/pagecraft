Operating model

Each hub handles intake, scheduled maintenance, and routine visits. Complex cases remain with the central engineering team. Shared dispatch keeps coverage balanced during leave and seasonal demand.

Monthly reporting will track customer response, unresolved cases, travel hours, and staffing capacity. The regional manager will review exceptions with the service director.

04 · RISK & GOVERNANCE

Controls for a phased launch

The second hub proceeds only after the Cuenca review confirms service quality, hiring readiness, and spending within the approved limit.

Risks and response

Hiring takes longer than planned. Start recruiting before the lease is signed and use temporary Quito coverage during onboarding.

Demand is below forecast. Use flexible workspace and hold the Loja launch until the Cuenca review confirms sufficient customer volume.

Service quality falls during transition. Keep escalation ownership central and review unresolved cases every week for the first 90 days.

Approval requested
Authorize up to $75,000 for the Cuenca launch. Release the remaining Loja investment after the June decision gate.
